## Pedagogical Architecture

1. **"Learn by Doing" Learning Pyramid:**
   - 75% retention through *Practice by Doing* and 90% through *Teaching Others* (vs. 5% for passive lecture).
   - **Effectual Reasoning:** Students connect available clinical and laboratory means ($M_1 \dots M_5$) to novel implant opportunities ($E_1 \dots E_5$).

2. **Synchronized Problem-Based (PBL) & Simulation-Based Learning (SBL) Cycles:**
   - **DO (Concrete Experience):** Simulated surgical reaming, osteotomy, and internal fixation.
   - **OBSERVE (Reflective Observation):** Debriefing clinical nuances, ergonomics, and bone-implant stability.
   - **THINK (Abstract Conceptualization):** Modeling contact mechanics, stress shielding, and osseointegration.
   - **PLAN (Active Experimentation):** Prototyping custom implant modifications and benchtop mechanical validation.

## 15-Week Curriculum & Core Surgical Activities (A–G)

- **Week 1:** Introduction & Virtual Hip Surgery (HTC Vive VR + 3D Systems Touch Haptic Robotic Force-Feedback)
- **Week 2 [Activity A]:** Build Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal Model (Synthetic skeleton + anatomic foam muscle groups)
- **Week 3 [Activity B]:** Arthroscopic ACL Reconstruction (Tibial/femoral tunnel drilling & interference screws)
- **Week 4 [Activity C]:** Total Hip Arthroplasty / THA (Femoral neck resection, canal broaching, press-fit stem)
- **Week 5 [Activity D]:** Open Reduction Internal Fixation / ORIF (Dynamic compression plating & bicortical screws)
- **Week 6 [Activity E]:** Posterior Lumbar Spine Stabilization (Pedicle cannulation & contouring cobalt-chrome rods)
- **Week 7:** Bone Material Testing (Synthetic cortical & cancellous mechanical properties)
- **Week 8 [Activity F]:** Dental Implants & Osseodensification (Versah Densah® burs & insertion torque testing)
- **Week 9 [Activity G]:** Patient-Specific 3D Cranioplasty Skull Plate (Materialise Mimics CT segmentation & 3D printing)
- **Week 10:** Opportunity Creation Project (PBL Clinician-Engineer collaboration with Centrepolis Accelerator)
- **Weeks 11–15:** Advanced Seminars (FEA Biomechanical Modeling, Orthotics Industry Tour, Sports Medicine Clinic Tour, Hospital Tour, Concussion Seminar).
